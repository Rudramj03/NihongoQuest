💡 The golden rules

  • Consistency beats intensity — 20 minutes daily outperforms 3 hours on Sunday.
  • Audio first — always hear a word when you learn it. Every word on this site is speakable.
  • Output early — say sentences aloud, even alone. Shadowing the example audio counts.
  • Games keep you coming back — your XP and streak on the Progress page are the real metric.
